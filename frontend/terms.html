Terms and Conditions

Last updated: October 13, 2026

These terms describe expected use of GPB-Resolve, a college problem reporting and resolution application for the GP Banka campus. The site owner should review them before publication.

Use of the service

Use the application to report genuine campus concerns, follow their progress, and carry out authorized administrative review. You must provide accurate account information and keep your sign-in credentials private. Email verification may be required to register or sign in.

Complaint submissions

Submit clear, accurate, and respectful descriptions that are relevant to a campus issue. Select the closest available category and provide a useful location. Only upload images you have the right to share and that are relevant to the complaint. Complaint images are limited by the application’s configured upload rules.

Prohibited use

Do not use GPB-Resolve to submit spam, knowingly false reports, threats, abusive or unlawful content, malicious files, or information that you do not have permission to disclose. Do not attempt to access another person's account or complaint, bypass access controls, disrupt the service, or use administrative functions without authorization. Access to administrator accounts is subject to approval by an existing administrator.

Review and resolution

Administrators may review, assign, update the status of, and add resolution notes to complaints. Submission does not guarantee a particular outcome or response time. The application does not currently publish a service-level agreement or guaranteed resolution timeframe. Users can view available status updates by signing in.

Availability and maintenance

The service may be unavailable during maintenance or due to network, hosting, or other technical issues. Keep your own copy of information you may need. The application is provided as a campus workflow tool; use the college's established channels for urgent safety matters or emergencies.

Questions and site owner review

For questions about these terms, contact the GPB-Resolve site administrator through the contact channel designated by GP Banka. No verified contact details are currently configured on this website.

Site owner review required: Confirm the responsible institution, complaint-handling expectations, acceptable-use enforcement process, and administrator contact channel before treating these terms as final or legally binding.
